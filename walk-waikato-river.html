Waikato River Walk

About this walk

The Waikato River is the longest river in New Zealand (425 km) and it runs right through the middle of Hamilton. There are walking paths on both sides of the river so you can walk as far as you like.

In our video we walk along the river path, past the Central City Jetty and down onto the sandy banks where the ducks hang out. We filmed it in October so there is lots of blossom on the trees.

Video description: A 5 minute walk along the Waikato River in Hamilton. It starts in the clear shallows at the edge of the river, follows the path through gardens full of blossom, goes down onto a sandy bank where geese are standing, and finishes at the Central City Jetty.

Things to look out for

  • The clear water near the edge, you can see the stones on the bottom
  • The Central City Jetty
  • Ducks and geese on the sand banks
  • Pink and red flowers on the trees along the path

Location: Central Hamilton

Time: Walk as far as you want

Tips: The path is flat and sealed so it's good for prams and bikes too.
