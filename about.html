What WaiView Is

WaiView is a website where you can watch walks around the Waikato. Each walk has a video, a map and some information about the area, so you can see what it's like before you visit.

What We Do

We are a group of first year IT students at Wintec in Hamilton. We picked walks that are close to home and filmed them ourselves. We are planning to add more walks soon.

Our Logo and Colours

Our logo is meant to look like a river walk. The blue wave is the water and the footsteps go from "Waikato" to "View". Wai means water in te reo Māori, so WaiView means "water view".

Our colours come from things you see on a walk. Green for the ground, blue for water, cream for clouds and light blue for the sky.
