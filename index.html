Our Mission

WaiView lets you explore walks around the Waikato from home. We film the walks so you can follow along, see the scenery and learn a bit about each place before you go there yourself.

Who is it for?

We made WaiView so anyone can enjoy a walk around the Waikato, whatever their situation.

  • People with disabilities - not every track is easy to get to, and some people just can't do a long walk. Our videos let you experience the whole walk from home, at your own pace. Every video has captions that describe what is on screen and a written description, the site works with screen readers and keyboards, and you can switch to a dark or high contrast colour theme at the bottom of every page.
  • People who are unwell, recovering or getting older - if you can't get out on the track right now, you can still see the lakes and the river and enjoy being outside for a bit.
  • Students and teachers - each walk has history, a stop-by-stop guide, facts about the plants and birds, and questions to talk about, so it works well for learning about local places in class or at home.
  • People planning a visit - watch the walk first to see what the track is like, how long it takes and whether it suits prams or wheelchairs, then go and do it yourself.
  • Anyone who wants to relax - put a walk on, sit back and enjoy the view.
